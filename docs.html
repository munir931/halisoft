CameraScan Pro Documentation


1. Installation

Search for "CameraScan Pro" on the Play Store or download the APK from the link below. Ensure you allow CAMERA_PERMISSION for the scanner to function.

2. How to Scan

Point your camera at any document. The AI Edge Detection will highlight the paper in green. Tap the shutter to capture. The app will automatically perspective-warp the image to a flat PDF.

3. Premium Features

To remove the "Scanned with HaliSoft" watermark, navigate to Settings > Remove Watermark. You can unlock this for free by watching a short video ad.
